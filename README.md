# Gymmetry 

**A personal workout tracker that you can freely customize with ease.**
*It's never too late to shape up!*

### [Open Gymmetry](https://taraalleah.github.io/Gymmetry/)

Works in any browser, and is made to be used on a phone. Open the link and use
"Add to Home Screen" to keep it like an app.

## What you can do

- Build your own workouts with a warm-up, exercises by **sets & reps** or by **timer**, and optional **extra** exercises for days with more energy
- Mark exercises as weighted 
- Work out with a small stopwatch running in the corner
- Rate your energy with stars, tick off your step goal and write a short journal note
- See your progress on a calendar, plus your streak and monthly average energy
- Pick your own mascot shape (circle, square, triangle, star or flower) and colour
- Choose Light, Dark or Soft pastel mode

## Your data

Everything is saved in your own browser. There are no accounts and nothing is sent anywhere.
That also means your history stays on the device you used, and clearing your browser data will erase it.

## Run it yourself

Built with React and Vite.

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

To publish your own copy, push to GitHub and set **Settings → Pages → Source** to **GitHub Actions**.
The included workflow builds and deploys it for you.
